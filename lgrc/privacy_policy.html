Privacy Policy

1. Principles of Data Collection

Our Service, the Smart LG TV Remote Controller Chrome extension, is built on the principle of privacy-by-design. We strictly utilize anonymized data collection and do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, or physical address.

2. Scope and Purpose of Collected Data

During your use of the Service, we process only technical and strictly anonymized data for the following specific purposes:

  • Subscription Management: We use an anonymous identifier token linked to your installation to verify your active subscription status and grant access to premium features.
  • Usage Analytics and Feature Efficiency: We track anonymous usage patterns (e.g., which features are used most frequently) to understand how the application is utilized and to evaluate the effectiveness of specific functions.
  • Error Tracking and Diagnostics: We collect anonymous crash reports and error logs to identify bugs, troubleshoot technical issues, and ensure the stability of the application.
  • Future Development: The aggregated, anonymous statistical data guides our development roadmap, helping us improve the user experience and introduce new features based on actual usage trends.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize third-party infrastructure to facilitate the Service:

  • Payment Processing: Subscriptions are securely processed via third-party payment providers (like Stripe). When you initiate a payment, you interact directly with their secure systems. They handle your billing information according to their own privacy policies. We only receive an anonymous confirmation of your subscription status.

4. Data Security and Retention

All anonymous data collected is transmitted over secure channels and stored on protected servers. We retain this technical data only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above (e.g., maintaining your active subscription and analyzing general performance trends), after which it is routinely purged.

5. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or technical updates. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally. Continued use of the application signifies your agreement to the current Privacy Policy.
